# IMS / Fitness Quest 10 — public competitor audit (2026-09-23)

Scope: Publicly advertised services and review themes; not a claim about FQ10's internal coaching quality, client outcomes, private data, or individual staff. Jason's firsthand perspective: FQ10 emphasizes community, fun and strength; IMS intends a quieter, more introvert-friendly, genuinely individualized experience with joint health at the center.

## Public evidence
- FQ10 official homepage: https://fitnessquest10.com/ — community-first messaging, personal training, classes, memberships, massage/stretching, HYROX, free consultation. Three advertised locations: South, North, Pacific Beach.
- FQ10 official services: https://fitnessquest10.com/services/ — individualized personal training, flexibility, Pilates/yoga, corrective movements, joint health claims, bodywork/FST, sports performance. Do NOT claim they do not address joints.
- FQ10 official HYROX: https://fitnessquest10.com/hyroxtraining/ — Wednesday North and Sunday South group sessions advertised when checked.
- FQ10 ClassPass: https://classpass.com/studios/fitness-quest-10-san-diego-oavt — multiple daily group sessions, positive class ratings; listings and prices can change.
- Third-party recent public review aggregation: https://www.loc8nearme.com/california/san-diego/fitness-quest-10/2839489/ — recent reviews highlight family atmosphere, support, positivity and community.
- IMS website: https://imsmethod.com/ — joint-by-joint assessment, one-coach continuity, quiet private space, FRC/FRA/Kinstretch and recovery. Website copy should be checked against real operating capacity as IMS adds trainers.

## Positioning distinction (service design, not claims of competitor deficiency)
FQ10 public promise: motivating community and fun, group strength/conditioning and broad services. They ALSO advertise personalized training, joint health and recovery; do not falsely claim these are absent.
IMS proposed promise: measured joint-by-joint assessment before programming; private/low-stimulation sessions; continuity with a named coach; specific mobility and strength milestones; documented progression; recovery integrated with training; client can choose how much social interaction they want.

## Proposed IMS website/app experience
1. Landing page two paths: 'Private coaching' and 'Explore services', with one primary CTA: Book a movement consultation.
2. Intake asks: 'Preferred training environment: quiet/private / some social energy / group atmosphere / no preference'; never infer introversion or psychological state.
3. Coach OS Today screen leads with sessions, client assessments, next training blocks and recovery flags; finance is separate owner-only Business Overview.
4. Client program home leads with today's movement focus, approved workout, coach note and progress in strength, mobility and conditioning; no public leaderboards by default.
5. Publish evidence-backed educational pages for hip/shoulder/knee movement assessment and training, without claiming to diagnose or treat injuries.
6. Content proof: coach-filmed real exercise demos, consented client testimonials, assessment/process walkthroughs, accurate case outcomes; no AI-generated technique demonstration without coach verification.
7. Acquisition measurement: Google Business Profile visits → booking CTA clicks → booked consults → attended consults → retained clients. Don't confuse profile searches or direction requests with booked clients.
8. Do not use former employer's confidential information, nonpublic client lists or private operating procedures.

## Competitive hypotheses to test, not facts
- Quiet appointment-based coaching may attract adults who avoid busy class floors.
- Showing objective joint metrics may make assessment-led training easier to understand.
- Clear recovery and joint-control explanations may increase consult conversions.
