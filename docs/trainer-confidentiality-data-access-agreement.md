## 1. Purpose and authorized access
IMS uses a shared-care training model. Approved trainers may access IMS client records when reasonably necessary to deliver, coordinate, document, or improve authorized IMS services. Access does not authorize personal browsing, use for outside businesses, or sharing information with unauthorized people.

## 2. Confidential client information
Confidential information includes client contact details, assessments, injury and health information voluntarily supplied to IMS, movement screens, training plans, workout logs, progress photographs and videos, communications, scheduling details, and payment-related information available through IMS systems. The trainer will access and use only the information needed for legitimate IMS work and will follow client permissions and IMS policies.

## 3. Handling and communication
The trainer will use their individual IMS account, enable multifactor authentication when offered, keep credentials private, lock unattended devices, and use IMS-approved communication and storage systems. The trainer will not download, screenshot, forward, upload to personal cloud storage, enter into unapproved AI tools, or publish client information without IMS authorization and any necessary client consent. Exercise media featuring identifiable clients requires specific permission before publication.

## 4. Security incidents and concerns
The trainer will promptly notify the IMS owner or designated privacy contact of suspected lost devices, mistaken disclosures, account compromise, inappropriate access, or other incidents. The trainer will cooperate with containment and follow IMS incident-response instructions. IMS will assess notification obligations under applicable law.

## 5. Role boundaries
Shared access to client programs and assessments does not grant authority to change staff roles, alter system security settings, export the client database, change financial administration, or approve exercises outside the trainer's qualifications and IMS review process. IMS may audit access and restrict privileges where appropriate.

## 6. Ending access
When the trainer leaves IMS or no longer needs access, IMS may revoke the account immediately. The trainer will return or securely delete IMS information in their possession as directed, subject to any legal retention obligations, and will continue to protect confidential information afterward.

## 7. Acknowledgment
I have read and understand these responsibilities, will complete required IMS onboarding, and will ask the IMS owner when uncertain whether a use or disclosure is permitted. I understand that IMS may enforce its access and confidentiality policies subject to applicable law.
