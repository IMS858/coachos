# IMS Health Data Boundary

Apple Health / HealthKit evidence is optional client coaching context.

Allowed product use:
- the same client's Progress and Fuel & Performance views;
- assigned coach/owner review for that client's training, recovery and nutrition conversations;
- client-visible summaries and same-client longitudinal trends;
- source-aware weight/activity/sleep/workout context.

Prohibited product use:
- Growth Center, Research Desk, lead scoring, prospect qualification or marketing segmentation;
- advertising, lookalike audiences, retargeting or campaign attribution;
- selling, licensing or disclosing HealthKit-derived evidence to data brokers;
- diagnosing conditions, medical treatment decisions, or automatic nutrition/program changes;
- treating missing samples as zero, failure, denial of permission, or noncompliance;
- treating Watch active-energy estimates as measured energy expenditure for automatic calorie prescriptions;
- treating home BIA as interchangeable with IMS Bod Pod measurements.

Data minimization:
- no clinical records;
- no raw heart-rate stream;
- no routes/location;
- no nutrition intake import;
- no reproductive/symptom data;
- no HealthKit writes in the current integration.

Bod Pod remains the IMS body-composition anchor. Home scales/Hume remain source-identified trend evidence.
