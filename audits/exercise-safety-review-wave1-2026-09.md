## Mandatory IMS coach checklist

1. Confirm the exercise identity, variant, equipment, starting position, and intended range of motion. Record the canonical ID and the matched Coach OS entry.
2. Verify primary and secondary joints, loading demands, setup and coaching cues against the actual movement. Populate missing primary-joint metadata.
3. Review contraindications and restrictions, especially active flare, post-surgery, avoid-loading, spinal red flags, balance and cardio-intensity clearance. A missing contraindication tag is **unknown**, not cleared.
4. Document an acceptable regression/substitution only after checking its own approved tags and the individual client's assessment. Never substitute by name similarity alone.
5. Record reviewer identity, rationale and approval time. Re-review after any safety-critical edit or canonical remap.
6. Do not enable client visibility or generator publishing until the explicit release checklist is complete.

## Image and video quality standard

Higgsfield may help produce **branding, thumbnails, UI concepts and non-instructional visuals**. For exercise technique and joint-position guidance, use verified coach-recorded reference footage or a coach-approved storyboard. Generated imagery can invent limb positions, apparatus details, ranges of motion and contraindication cues; it must never be treated as movement-safety evidence. Clearly distinguish stylized marketing art from actual instruction.
